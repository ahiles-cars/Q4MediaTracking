Week 1 - Pipeline Created = MRR of open-stage opportunities created Oct 26–30, 2026. Week 2 = the same measure for Nov 2–6, 2026 (complete). Week 3 = the same measure for Nov 9–13, 2026; it is partial, because the export includes created dates only through Nov 12.

Logic and rules for this table
  • Scope: opportunities from the Pipeline tab: Close Date Oct 1 – Dec 31, 2026; stages Closed Won, Committed to Close, Follow Up, Closing, Presentation and Discovery; trial quotes excluded; Media only (by forecast product tower when there is no quote or no quote lines, otherwise by product tower on the quote lines).
  • Opportunity MRR: the sum of Incremental MRR across the opportunity’s product lines. Negative lines net against it.
  • Q4 media sold: sum of MRR where Stage = Closed Won. Includes net new customers, upgrades, downgrades and partial cancellations; full cancellations are excluded.
  • Q4 open pipeline: sum of MRR where Stage is Committed to Close, Follow Up, Closing, Presentation or Discovery.
  • Week 1 - Pipeline Created: “Created Active Pipeline”: MRR of open-stage opportunities (the same five stages) with a Created Date of Oct 26–30, 2026. Closed Won is not included.
  • Week 2 - Pipeline Created: the same definition as Week 1 for a Created Date of Nov 2–6, 2026. Week 3 is the same definition for a Created Date of Nov 9–13, 2026 (partial through Nov 12). Each refresh adds the next Monday–Friday week as a new column.
  • Roll-up: Field East, Field West, Inside and Majors come from the data’s region column; Field East and West roll up into the Field channel. Managers come from the data’s Manager Category column (Inside shows as one team named “Inside”).
  • Manager rows: in the all-managers view, each manager is listed under Field East and Field West (largest open pipeline first), and their rows add up to the region line. Inside and Majors have no manager breakdown.
  • Manager filter: shows only that manager’s opportunities; totals recalculate and the manager rows are hidden.

The sales manager filter re-cuts the headline tiles, the pipeline table and the detail table. The team and created by filters apply to the detail table.

Logic and rules for this table
  • Scope: the same opportunities as the pipeline table, one row per opportunity, MRR summed across its product lines.
  • Stages shown: open pipeline only (Committed to Close, Follow Up, Closing, Presentation, Discovery), meaning deals still scheduled to close. Closed Won is not listed here.

  • Team filter: Field East, Field West, Inside and Majors from the data’s region column.
  • Created by filter: limited to Michelle McKenna, Shelley Rutherford, April Schenk, Ryan Altier and Ryan Kwasneski, using the opportunity’s CreatedByName. “All creators” shows every creator.
